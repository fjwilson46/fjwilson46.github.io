Money Ledger Sample

Three findings from one made up company, Harbor Street Goods. Each number shows where it came from, what it assumes, and what it does not mean. It runs in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

How to read this page. Money is kept in four separate lines: invoiced spend, estimated labor, potential exposure, and verified outcomes. The lines are never added together. A blank is shown as "Not estimated", never as zero. Time that could be freed up is not the same as cash saved.

The ledger

Change it and watch the estimated labor line move. The invoiced line does not move, because it comes from invoices.
