DUCHESS DON'T PLAY Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026. Made by Future ARC Studios.

The short version

This game does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no purchases.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (levels cleared, stars, coins, settings) on your own device, in the game's local storage. It never leaves your device. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the game, that progress is gone.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or contact details.
  • No location.
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.
  • No photos, microphone, or camera access.

Children

The game has no chat, no social features, no ads, and no links that lead players away from the game. Because nothing is collected, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Third party services

If you play on a website or store such as itch.io or Google Play, that service may collect its own information under its own privacy policy. Future ARC Studios does not receive that information.

Changes

If we ever add online features such as accounts, leaderboards, or ads, we will update this policy first and tell players in the game.
