Job Posting Grabber Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version

Job Posting Grabber keeps everything on your own computer. It does not send your data to me or to anyone else.

What the extension reads

When you click the extension icon, it reads the title, company name, and web address of the page in your current tab. It reads the page only at that moment, and only the tab you are on.

What the extension stores

When you click Save, it stores the title, company, web address, the status you pick (Saved, Applied, or Skipped), your optional note, and the date and time, in your browser's local extension storage. This data stays on your device.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send data to any server.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share data.
  • It does not run on a page until you click its icon.
  • It does not submit, fill in, or change anything on a web page.

Your control

You can export your list to a CSV file at any time with the Export CSV button. Removing the extension, or clearing your browser's site data, erases the stored list.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: read the title, company, and address of the tab you are on when you click the icon.
  • storage: keep your saved list on your device.
  • alarms: refresh the weekly count shown on the icon.
