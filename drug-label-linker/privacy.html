Drug Label Linker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

We collect nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, or tracking. Nothing leaves your browser.

What the extension reads

To find medicine names, the extension reads the text of web pages you open, on your own device. Page text is processed there and is not saved or sent to anyone. It skips password fields, inputs, text boxes, and code blocks.

What the extension stores

The only saved data is your on or off switch and your list of skipped sites. These settings use local extension storage and stay on your device. The extension does not use sync storage and does not keep a browsing log.

Links you click

Links to DailyMed and MedlinePlus open only when you click them. Clicking the DailyMed link sends the name you clicked to DailyMed as part of its search address. Opening either site is normal browser navigation, and those sites have their own privacy policies. The extension does not control what those sites collect.

What the extension does not do

  • It makes no network requests of its own.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share data.
  • It gives no medical advice. It points to official sources only.

Your control

You can remove a site from the skipped list in the toolbar popup. Removing the extension removes its stored settings.

Permissions

  • storage: saves the switch and the skipped site list on your device.
  • Content script on web pages: reads page text on your device to find medicine names and add underlines.
